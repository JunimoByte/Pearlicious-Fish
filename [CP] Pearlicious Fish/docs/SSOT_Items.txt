Mollusk Meat – 30g, base drop from cracked shells, used for crafting torches and pathways
Pried Clam – Recycling Machine (Clam or Cockle, 2 hrs), cracked at Clint's for pearls and meat
Pried Oyster – Recycling Machine (Oyster, 2 hrs), cracked at Clint's for pearls and meat

Common Pearls (30% drop, Universal Like) – Small Pink, Small Purple, Small Rose Gold (150g); Pink, Purple (200g)
Rare Pearls (5.5% drop, Universal Like) – Purple Edison (300g), Pink Edison (350g), Purple Rose Edison (350g), Silver Edison (400g), Rose Gold (750g)
Legendary Pearls (1% drop, Universal Love) – Melo Pearl (3,000g, Clam only), Black Tahitian Pearl (4,000g), Peacock Tahitian Pearl (4,000g)

Royal Fang – Dagger, Combat level 8 recipe (5 Gold Bars, 1 Pearl, 2 Black Tahitian Pearls), 54–64 dmg, +4 speed, 9% crit
Sea King's Maul – Club, Combat level 9 recipe (5 Gold Bars, 1 Pearl, 2 Melo Pearls), 78–104 dmg, -4 speed, +3 defense, 28 AoE
Siren's Saber – Sword, Combat level 10 recipe (5 Gold Bars, 1 Pearl, 2 Peacock Tahitian Pearls), 70–84 dmg, +5 speed, +2 defense, 3% crit
Siren's Signet Ring – Ring (+1 Luck), Fishing level 10 recipe (5 Gold Bars, 3 Pearls, 2 Purple Rose Edison Pearls) or Clint's quest reward

Mermaid Silver Torch – Fishing level 5 recipe, 10 Mollusk Meat + 1 Purple Pearl
Mermaid Orb Torch – Fishing level 5 recipe, 10 Mollusk Meat + 1 Pink Pearl
Mermaid Path – Carpenter's Shop recipe (1,000g, fishing level 10+), crafted with 1 Mollusk Meat (yields 5 paths, +0.1 speed buff)
